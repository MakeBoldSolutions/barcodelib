# Brand guidelines — quick reference

**Make Bold Solutions** — fractional CFO services for small/midsize businesses, sole proprietors, and nonprofits. "Big Firm Expertise. Small Firm Heart."

## Voice
Warm, senior, accessible — a seasoned CPA who partners with you, not a hype deck. Speaks as "we" to "you". Short, declarative, parallel sentences; triads are common ("cash flow clarity, a budget you can use, books you can trust"). Sentence case throughout, except the signature spaced-caps "S O L U T I O N S" treatment for eyebrows/labels — never for paragraphs. No emoji. Restrained punctuation; em dashes for asides.

Avoid buzzwords and hyperbole. Name the audience plainly (small businesses, sole proprietors, nonprofits). Credentials matter (CPA, PwC alumna, CFO of the Year).

## Color
- **Rust `#982407`** — primary. Logos, headlines, key surfaces. Use with conviction, not everywhere.
- **Ember `#C6620C`** — secondary accent. Highlights, links, hovers.
- **Ink `#1E1E1E`** — text and dark surfaces (warm near-black, not pure black).
- **Cream `#F8F6F2`** — default page surface. White is reserved for cards/elevated surfaces.
- Status colors are muted — finance-grade, never candy-bright.

## Type
- **Be Vietnam Pro** (display) — headlines, wordmark, brand moments. Extrabold/black for display, bold/semibold for headings.
- **Inter Tight** (body/UI) — long-form content, labels, controls.
- Headlines: tight tracking, line-height ~1.05–1.15. Body: roomy, line-height 1.55.

## Layout & surfaces
- 4px spacing grid. Generous section rhythm. Content max-width ~1200px, prose ~68ch.
- Modest corner radii (4–10px) — nothing bubbly; the mark is sharp and geometric.
- Cards: white on cream, hairline warm border, small-to-medium soft shadow.
- Shadows are warm-tinted and tight — restraint over drama.
- Motion is quick and purposeful (120–200ms), no bounce or overshoot.

## Avoid
Bluish-purple gradients, glassmorphism, neon, emoji, candy colors, heavy drop shadows, pill-everything UI, decorative gradient blobs.
